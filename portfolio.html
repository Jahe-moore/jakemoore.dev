Data Visualization

Orbit Congestion

Maps 70,000+ cataloged space objects to show how fast low Earth orbit is filling and who is filling it. It splits working satellites from rocket bodies and debris, and the orbit view puts the 35,000 objects still in orbit around Earth as dots. Pulled from CelesTrak's catalog with Python; the footer shows the pull date.

  • Skills: Data pipeline design, calculated fields, dashboard design, data storytelling
  • Tools: Tableau Public, Python, CelesTrak SATCAT
